# MetricOps Platform Overview

This outline is the editable source for the stakeholder presentation. It intentionally describes capabilities and outcomes without naming implementation technologies.

---

## Slide 1 — MetricOps

### Infrastructure assurance from one trusted workspace

Observe services. Detect risk. Coordinate response. Operate with accountability.

---

## Slide 2 — The operational problem

- Infrastructure information is spread across many consoles and teams.
- Managers lack a dependable organization-wide health view.
- Operators spend time correlating servers, applications, databases, and alerts.
- Important incidents and administrative actions are difficult to reconstruct.
- External support access is often either unavailable or too broadly trusted.

---

## Slide 3 — The MetricOps answer

One governed platform to:

- Discover and organize infrastructure.
- Understand service health and capacity.
- Monitor applications, databases, servers, and cloud resources.
- Detect and coordinate operational incidents.
- Produce availability and audit evidence.
- Perform only approved and supported actions.

---

## Slide 4 — A shared view for every role

### Leadership

Service availability, critical risks, trends, and accountability.

### IT management

Inventory, ownership, capacity, incidents, maintenance, and reporting.

### Operations teams

Detailed health, evidence, alerts, runbooks, and authorized actions.

### Auditors and security teams

Access reviews, findings, support sessions, changes, and retained evidence.

---

## Slide 5 — What customers can observe

- Server availability, utilization, storage, network, and uptime.
- Application reachability, response time, failures, and certificates.
- Database availability, connections, storage, performance, and backup posture.
- Cloud resources, service status, alarms, and configuration findings.
- Monitoring connection health and data freshness.

---

## Slide 6 — From signal to resolution

### Continuous operating loop

Observe → Detect → Acknowledge → Investigate → Act safely → Verify → Report → Improve

Every stage retains ownership, evidence, time, and outcome.

---

## Slide 7 — Clear, actionable dashboards

- Organization and critical-service health.
- Infrastructure and database capacity.
- Active alerts and incidents.
- Resources approaching operational limits.
- Security and resilience configuration findings.
- Recent maintenance, support, and administrative activity.
- Reports designed for both management and technical teams.

---

## Slide 8 — Safe operational control

Sensitive actions follow a controlled path:

1. Confirm identity.
2. Check permission and resource capability.
3. Evaluate environment policy.
4. Obtain approval where required.
5. Execute a defined action.
6. Verify the resulting state and health.
7. Preserve the complete audit record.

No unrestricted remote command access.

---

## Slide 9 — Two administration planes

### MetricOps platform administration

Manages customers, subscriptions, deployments, entitlements, versions, and support cases.

### Customer administration

Manages customer users, environments, connections, resources, policies, alerts, incidents, and operations.

Platform administration does not automatically grant customer infrastructure access.

---

## Slide 10 — Customer-controlled support

- No customer telemetry or infrastructure access by default.
- Customer approves the exact support scope.
- Access is named, time-limited, and revocable.
- Active support sessions remain visible.
- Secrets are never displayed.
- Every support view and action is recorded.
- Isolated environments can use approved diagnostic bundles instead of remote access.

---

## Slide 11 — Security and trust by design

- Customer-controlled hosting options.
- Strong identity and least-privilege access.
- Separation of monitoring and operational authority.
- Protected credentials and encrypted communication.
- Read-only operation by default.
- Explainable findings instead of unsupported claims.
- Complete administrative and operational accountability.

---

## Slide 12 — Designed for government operations

- Can run in a department or approved government environment.
- Supports private and disconnected infrastructure patterns.
- Provides predictable reporting and audit evidence.
- Separates provider support from departmental authority.
- Supports retention, access review, backup, recovery, and operational procedures.
- Scales from one department pilot to shared provincial services.

---

## Slide 13 — Focused first release

- Customer and platform administration.
- Server, endpoint, database, and selected cloud-resource monitoring.
- Resource inventory and ownership.
- Curated health dashboards.
- Alert, incident, and maintenance workflow.
- Availability, capacity, findings, and audit reports.
- Customer-approved support access.
- One controlled non-production operation after read-only acceptance.

---

## Slide 14 — Pilot proposal

### A measured 90-day operational pilot

- 20–50 servers.
- 3–5 important applications.
- One database environment.
- One connected cloud account where applicable.
- Agreed health checks and alert catalogue.
- Role-based training and operating procedures.
- Monthly availability, capacity, incident, and audit evidence.

---

## Slide 15 — Pilot outcomes

The pilot will measure:

- Infrastructure coverage and ownership.
- Service-check coverage and availability accuracy.
- Time to detect and acknowledge incidents.
- Alert quality and reduction of noise.
- Capacity risks identified before failure.
- Completeness of privileged-action evidence.
- Time saved preparing operational reports.
- Trust and usability across management, operations, and audit roles.

---

## Slide 16 — The result

### One trusted operational picture

MetricOps helps organizations know what they operate, understand what needs attention, coordinate the response, and prove what happened—without surrendering control of their infrastructure.

### Recommended next step

Select one department, one non-critical service group, and measurable pilot outcomes.
